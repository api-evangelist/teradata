Teradata announced that Josh Fecteau has assumed the combined role of Chief Data and AI Officer and Chief Information Officer (CDAO and CIO), unifying the company's data, AI, and technology leadership under a single executive. Fecteau had previously served as Chief Data and AI Officer after joining Teradata in late 2025. The consolidation reflects Teradata's strategy to align AI and data capabilities with its internal technology infrastructure.
